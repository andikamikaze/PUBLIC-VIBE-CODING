Understanding BMI

Body Mass Index is a screening tool that uses height and weight to estimate body fatness. It's calculated as:

weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²

Standard categories: Underweight (<18.5), Healthy (18.5–24.9), Overweight (25–29.9), Obesity (30+).

What BMI Cannot Tell You

  • Muscle mass and body composition
  • Where body fat is distributed
  • Age, genetics, and medical history
  • Overall fitness and nutrition

Consult a healthcare professional for personalized advice.
